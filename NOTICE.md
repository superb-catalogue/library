# Notices this library must carry

Most of what is here is public domain: the books themselves, each with its
own `provenance.json` recording the edition it came from and the terms it
arrived under. Those need no notice beyond the record beside them.

One kind of file here is different, and this page exists so that difference
is written down once rather than left to be worked out from a script.

## The gloss tables (`books/<id>/glosses.json`)

Each book carries a `glosses.json` beside its `book.json`: the word meanings
for that book's own vocabulary, so the reading app can answer a tapped word.
They are generated by `data/pipeline/book_glosses.py` in
[kihea/superb](https://github.com/kihea/superb), and they are **not** public
domain.

The definitions come from the English Wiktionary, extracted with Tatu
Ylonen's `wiktextract` (MIT-licensed code, published by kaikki.org). The
extracted text is Wiktionary's own content and carries Wiktionary's licence:
**CC BY-SA 4.0, or the GFDL, at your choice.** That is a share-alike
obligation, accepted deliberately rather than by accident.

What this means in practice:

- **Credit.** Wiktionary contributors are the authors of these definitions.
  The reading app carries that credit where the definitions reach a reader,
  under Settings, and anyone else redistributing these files owes the same
  credit.
- **Pass on the same freedom.** A modified gloss table stays under CC BY-SA
  4.0 or the GFDL.
- **It does not spread.** The obligation binds these files, not the books
  beside them and not this repository's scripts. A book's text is still
  under whatever its own `provenance.json` states, and the scripts and notes
  here are still MIT. Running a script over a dataset does not make the
  result the script's; it also does not make the rest of the repository the
  dataset's.

They are kept here rather than in the app's repository because a book and
its word meanings are one thing, and the app fetches both from the same
commit. Moving a file does not move its obligations off it, so the
obligation moved here with them.

The app's own ledger of every dataset it uses, and which of them ship, is
`data/MANIFEST.md` in [kihea/superb](https://github.com/kihea/superb), with
the full notices in `data/NOTICE.md` beside it.
